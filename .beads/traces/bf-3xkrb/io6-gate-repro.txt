ROOT-CAUSE NOTE FOR THE FIX CHILDREN (no fix applied here — diagnostic-only bead)
================================================================================
The sim OBTAINS a token from /api/provision ("Auto-provisioned token: 8364555a..."),
presents it on the WS dial, and the ingestion token-validator (main.go SetTokenValidator,
HMAC-SHA256(installSecret, mac)) REJECTS it as invalid. So the provisioned token and the
validated token do not agree. Two leading hypotheses for the fix bead to confirm:
  (a) MAC mismatch: /api/provision was called without the node's real MAC (the plan's
      /api/provision spec mints a placeholder token when `mac` is absent), so the token is
      HMAC(secret, <placeholder>) while hello carries the real MAC AA:BB:CC:00:00:00 ->
      HMAC mismatch -> "invalid token".
  (b) install-secret source divergence: /api/provision and the validator read the secret
      from different sources/paths, or the secret is regenerated between provision and
      validate.
